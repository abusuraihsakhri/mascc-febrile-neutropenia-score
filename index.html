MASCC Risk Index

Validated seven-component index (0–26 points). Higher scores indicate lower complication risk.

Clinical safety: Fever with neutropenia warrants prompt assessment and empiric treatment. A score ≥21 is not, by itself, a discharge recommendation; clinical stability, comorbidities, follow-up and logistics must also be assessed.
